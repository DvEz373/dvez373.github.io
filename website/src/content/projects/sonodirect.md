blurb: An AI-assisted IoT system that tunes room sound for small businesses.
problem: Small venues set up speakers by ear, which leaves uneven sound and distortion.
approach: ESP32 boards with MEMS microphones stream room measurements; machine-learning models predict better speaker placement; a Flutter web dashboard monitors and adjusts the setup.
result: About 19% better acoustic accuracy, 12% less distortion and 23% faster setup in the project's tests.
stack: [ESP32, MEMS microphones, Flutter, scikit-learn, TensorFlow]
